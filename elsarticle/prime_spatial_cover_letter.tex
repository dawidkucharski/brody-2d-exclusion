\opening{Dear Editor,}

Please consider the manuscript ``Paired spatial statistics under alternative
embeddings: exact graph-count variance and a prime-pattern application'' for
publication in \emph{Spatial Statistics}.

The paper addresses comparison of spatial summaries when the same selected
labels are mapped to different locations. A paired random-labelling procedure
is described, and an exact finite-population variance is derived for a
contrast of normalised edge counts on two simple graphs. The expression
incorporates graph overlap and differences in vertex degrees, covering
irregular open-window graphs as well as a simpler regular-toroidal case.
The derivation is checked by exhaustive small-graph enumeration and paired
simulations. A power-calibration study under planted gap-class enrichment,
with fixed Gaussian thresholds and an independent second-seed level check,
confirms that the paired procedure retains its nominal level and attributes
the planted structure to the embedding that carries it.

The method is illustrated using three embeddings of the same finite prime set.
Nearest-neighbour fits and established spatial summaries are compared with
fixed-count and mapped inhomogeneous nulls. The manuscript also reports the
substantial discrepancies between the continuous Brody fit and the tied
lattice-distance distributions, and limits its conclusions to the specified
models and finite windows.

The source code, numerical results and instructions needed to reproduce the
analysis and figures are supplied with the manuscript. AI-assisted code and
manuscript preparation are disclosed in the manuscript.

Thank you for considering the work.
