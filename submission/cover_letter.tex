\begin{letter}{The Editor\\Physica A: Statistical Mechanics and its Applications}

\opening{Dear Editor,}

I am pleased to submit our manuscript entitled ``The Brody exponent as a measure of exclusion strength in 2D spatial point processes: evidence from manufactured surfaces, prime embeddings, and interferometric profilometry'' for consideration for publication in Physica A.

\textbf{Why Physica A?} This paper develops a calibrated measurement framework for short-range exclusion in 2D spatial point processes---a statistical-physics methodology with applications spanning surface engineering, number theory, and optical metrology. The work bridges random matrix theory (Brody distribution) with established spatial point-process statistics, a topic well-suited to Physica A's interdisciplinary statistical-mechanics readership.

\textbf{Key contributions:}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item A necessary methodological correction: the 2D complete-spatial-randomness baseline for the Brody exponent is $\beta = 0.96 \pm 0.15$, not $\beta = 0$ as inherited from 1D applications.
  \item A monotonic, reproducible $\beta$--$r_{\text{excl}}/\langle\text{NN}\rangle$ calibration established through synthetic hard-core and soft-core point processes, translating the dimensionless Brody parameter into a physically interpretable exclusion radius.
  \item Rigorous density-thinning, embedding-robustness, spatial-permutation, and peak-detection sensitivity controls that isolate genuine exclusion from confounding effects.
  \item Application to three mechanistically distinct systems: 58 manufactured surface topographies, 2D embeddings of prime numbers, and phase-extracted interferometric profilometry of a certified roundness standard.
  \item A physical interpretation of the observed anti-clustering in prime embeddings: arithmetic divisibility constraints suppress short-range occupancy correlation, producing $\Delta D_2 > 0$ relative to density-matched random ensembles.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Rigour and reproducibility:} All analyses are accompanied by bootstrap confidence intervals, AIC-based model selection, Benjamini--Hochberg multiple-comparison correction, and power analysis. The complete 58-surface dataset, all analysis code, and a reproducible computational environment are archived in a public GitHub repository. Every numerical claim in the manuscript is verifiable against the deposited data.

\textbf{Declarations:} This manuscript is original, has not been published elsewhere, and is not under consideration by another journal. The author declares no competing interests. This is a single-author submission.

I confirm that the manuscript complies with Physica A's formatting and ethical guidelines.
